# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 28, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to addcn. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our component registry platform at addcn.dev (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information

- **Account Data**: Name, email address, and profile picture from GitHub or Google OAuth
- **Username**: Your chosen registry namespace

### Automatically Collected

- **Usage Data**: Features used, components published, and timestamps
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, and device identifiers

### User Content

- **Components**: Code and files you publish to your registry
- **Metadata**: Component names, descriptions, and dependencies

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the Service
- Authenticate your identity
- Process and serve your components
- Send important notifications
- Improve and develop the Service
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Information Sharing

We share your information with:

**Service Providers:**

- **Convex**: Database and backend services
- **Cloudflare R2**: Component file storage
- **GitHub/Google**: OAuth authentication

**Legal Requirements:**
We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Upon account deletion, your personal information and components will be removed within 90 days.

## 5. Your Rights

You may:

- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Export your component data

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including encryption, secure authentication, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure.

## 7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
